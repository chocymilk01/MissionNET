# Welcome to MissionNET

This blog documents my journey using professional two-way radios — primarily the Motorola MOTOTRBO family and Harris radios — in an amateur radio context.

## What is MissionNET?

MissionNET is an independent project aimed at sharing knowledge about professional radio systems with the amateur radio community. The goal is to bridge the gap between commercial/professional radio technology and amateur experimentation.

## What to Expect

Posts here will cover topics like:

- Getting started with MOTOTRBO radios on amateur frequencies
- DMR (Digital Mobile Radio) codeplug programming
- Harris radio configuration and capabilities
- Interoperability between different systems
- Tips, tools, and lessons learned

## Disclaimer

MissionNET is not affiliated with Motorola Solutions or Harris. All content is for educational and informational purposes only. Always operate within the terms of your amateur radio license and applicable regulations.
